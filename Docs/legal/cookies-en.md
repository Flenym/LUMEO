# Cookies & minor safety (EN, placeholder)

> Requires review by a lawyer before public release.

1. **Cookies/trackers.** Lumeo web surfaces (if any: landing, support) use strictly-necessary cookies only (session, banner consent). No advertising or cross-site trackers. Mobile apps do not use cookies; tokens live in the Keychain.
2. **Age 12+ & minor safety.** The service is 12+. Accounts showing as minors get hardened defaults: closed friend requests, hidden Last Seen, stricter public-field filters. Child-risk content gets priority moderation with fastest triage; where the law requires, it is reported to competent authorities.
3. **Parental controls.** A parent/guardian may request deletion or export of a minor's data via support; material terms changes require re-acceptance (see `terms-en.md`).
