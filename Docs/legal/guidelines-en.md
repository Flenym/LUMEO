# Lumeo Community Guidelines (EN, placeholder)

> Requires review by a lawyer before public release.

1. **Respect others:** no harassment, threats, discrimination, NSFW/violence, spam, fraud.
2. **Age 12+:** content must be appropriate for 12+; unsafe child content is banned and may be reported as required by law.
3. **Public is moderated:** statuses, profiles, Squad names, Workshop pass filters/moderation. Chats have no dictionary filter, but reports work: moderators see E2EE metadata only.
4. **Play fair:** no farming of XP/streaks/votes, no invite spam.
5. **Reports:** profile/message → Report; clustering speeds triage; false reports penalized.
6. **Enforcement:** warning → mute → ban; appeals via support.
